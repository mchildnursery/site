Our Approach

Montessori, practiced the way it was meant to be.

Every shelf, chair, and material in our home is sized, sequenced, and placed for one purpose: to let a child do things for themselves, at their own pace, without being rushed or rescued.

Independence First

Children pour, sweep, dress, and choose their own work — building real self-reliance, one small success at a time.

Hands-On Discovery

Concepts in math, language, and science are felt in the hands first — abstraction comes only once it's earned.

Respect for the Child

We observe before we intervene. Every guide follows the child's interest rather than a fixed lesson plan.

Prepared Environment

Low shelves, child-sized tools, and one material of each kind — a home built entirely to the child's scale.

Programs

Two communities, one continuous journey.

Children stay with the same mixed-age community for years, so younger ones learn by watching and older ones lead by teaching.

18 months – 3 years

Toddler Community

A gentle first step away from home — language-rich, movement-heavy, and centered on toilet learning, self-care, and first words.

  • Practical life & self-care routines
  • Sensorial exploration & fine motor work
  • Language immersion through songs & stories
3 – 6 years

Primary Community (Casa)

The classic Montessori "Children's House" — a full three-hour uninterrupted work cycle across practical life, sensorial, math, language, and culture.

  • Full Montessori material curriculum
  • Mixed-age peer teaching & leadership
  • Arabic, English & enrichment sessions
A Day Here

A rhythm children can set their own clock to.

Predictable, unhurried, and led by an uninterrupted block of work each morning — the heart of every Montessori day.

7:30

Arrival & Free Choice

Children settle in and choose their first piece of work from the shelf.

9:00

Circle Time

Songs, calendar, and a shared moment as a community.

9:30

The Work Cycle

An uninterrupted stretch of self-directed, hands-on learning.

11:30

Garden Time

Outdoor movement, sand, water, and real gardening tools.

12:15

Lunch & Rest

A family-style meal, followed by quiet rest or reading.

2:00

Afternoon Enrichment

Music, art, or movement before pickup begins.
